Methods

To calibrate the trait-labelled result against comparable lineages of the same tree, we compared its codeml branch-site test (model A against its omega2 = 1 null; PAML) result with every same-shaped label, using labelrank v0.5.0 (Kim 2026). The test set (a 2-tip clade with all its branches) was moved in turn to a uniform random sample (sampling seed 1) of 9 of the 27 possible sets of branches with the same shape lying entirely within the reference set (the unlabelled branches), excluding the two branches below the root; in each, the original test branches were returned to the background. Every label was analysed with the same codeml settings (PAML 4.10.10; CodonFreq = 2, cleandata = 0, each fit in its own folder) from the starting (omega, kappa) values (0.5, 2). For each label, each model was taken at its highest log-likelihood over the starts, LRT = 2(logL alternative - logL null), and P from the 50:50 mixture of 0 and chi-square(1) (the boundary null; the plain chi-square(1) is the conservative alternative). Runs that failed were excluded. The observed label's rank is 1 + the number of alternatives with LRT at or above it, out of N + 1, read over all alternatives and over the length-matched ones (total length within a factor of 2 of the test set's), since short lineages carry few substitutions and give small LRTs regardless of selection; the share of alternatives shorter than the test set and Spearman's correlation between length and LRT among the alternatives (LRTs below 0.01 counted as 0; given only when at least 3 alternatives reach it) are reported. The observed result counted as significant by the usual reading when its P <= 0.05. A rank-based P, rank / (N + 1), is reported only when N + 1 >= 20. Because the lineages of one tree differ in history and are not independent, the rank is a calibration against this tree's lineages, not an exact null distribution.

Results

The observed label gave LRT 0.00 (P 1 by the usual reading). It ranked 10 of 10 among the same-shaped labels read (9 alternatives at or above it), and 5 of 5 among the 4 length-matched ones; readings with N + 1 < 20 give a rank only. 78% of the alternatives were shorter than the test set; the length-LRT correlation was not computed (1 of 9 alternatives reached LRT 0.01). 0 of 9 alternatives (0%) would be called significant by the chi-square reading at P <= 0.05.
