Methods

To calibrate the trait-labelled result against comparable lineages of the same tree, we compared its HyPhy RELAX result with every same-shaped label, using labelrank v0.5.0 (Kim 2026). The test set (a 2-tip clade with all its branches) was moved in turn to each of the 12 possible sets of branches with the same shape lying entirely within the reference set (the branches labelled back), excluding the two branches below the root; in each, the original test branches were moved to a set that is neither test nor reference, so the command line was unchanged. Every label was analysed with the same command (HyPhy 2.5.101(MP), RELAX with the random seed fixed in the batch file, CPU=1) under seeds 1, 2, 3. For each label, the alternative and the null model were each taken at their highest log-likelihood over the seeds (for the alternative, over both of RELAX's fits when it refitted K from the other side of 1), and LRT = 2(logL alternative - logL null), with P from chi-square(1). Runs whose branch sets, as printed by HyPhy, differed by name from those written, and runs that failed, were excluded. The observed label's rank is 1 + the number of alternatives with LRT at or above it, out of N + 1, read over all alternatives and over the length-matched ones (total length within a factor of 2 of the test set's), since short lineages carry few substitutions and give small LRTs regardless of selection; the share of alternatives shorter than the test set and Spearman's correlation between length and LRT among the alternatives (LRTs below 0.01 counted as 0; given only when at least 3 alternatives reach it) are reported. The observed result counted as significant by the usual reading when its P <= 0.05. A rank-based P, rank / (N + 1), is reported only when N + 1 >= 20. Because the lineages of one tree differ in history and are not independent, the rank is a calibration against this tree's lineages, not an exact null distribution.

Results

The observed label gave LRT 7.23 (P 0.0072 by the usual reading). It ranked 1 of 13 among the same-shaped labels read (0 alternatives at or above it), and 1 of 3 among the 2 length-matched ones; readings with N + 1 < 20 give a rank only. 92% of the alternatives were shorter than the test set; across all 12 alternatives, length and LRT had Spearman's rho 0.15. 0 of 12 alternatives (0%) would be called significant by the chi-square reading at P <= 0.05.
